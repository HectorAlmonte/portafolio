# Surface brief: portfolio site (all routes)

Mode: Persuade (home) / Read (about, project detail) / Operate (contact form). Audience: recruiters and businesses, equally. Action: download CV, open a project, send a message.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a landscape at golden hour over the southern Peruvian sierra, painted in flat layered silhouettes like a national-park poster. Scrolling descends through the layers of the scene, sky to forest floor to night. Refuses the dark terminal/neon developer portfolio and the split hero with a badge row.

OWN-WORLD: Peach and cream sky (#fbe3c4 / #f6c69a), coral and salmon ridges (#ef8e6c / #e0705a), rust midground (#b8493f), plum and aubergine foreground (#5a1f33 / #2c0f1e), white-blue cloud highlights (#a9c6e8). Bold uppercase geometric display (Josefin Sans 700), friendly humanist body (Figtree). Sections meet on silhouette edges (ridgelines, treelines), never on hairlines or cards. Controls are rounded pills in plum on light grounds and in cream on dark ones.

STORY: A visitor reads "Full-stack developer" over the scene, sees who and where (Hector Almonte, Ilo, Peru), and gets the CV or the projects from the first viewport. Below, they see what sets him apart (industrial software in production, end to end, field/offline, supervision), the real projects, the timeline, and close at night with a contact call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A rounded framed scene with a cream margin, as in the reference. Nav inside the frame top: HA mountain mark left, links + ES/EN + CV right. Headline FULL-STACK DEVELOPER centered at roughly 9vw, sandwiched between parallax layers so the front treeline overlaps its baseline. Under it, name and location, two pill actions (projects, CV). Social links bottom-right inside the frame, in cream on plum.

FORM: Layered parallax landscape poster (user-pinned reference image). Signature interaction: the layers drift at different depths on scroll, the sun sinks, birds glide across; pointer adds a slight depth tilt on fine pointers. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out, entrances already visible by default, prefers-reduced-motion freezes the scene. seed: none (unavailable) — direction pinned by the user's reference image, no roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
