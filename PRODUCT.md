# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** (Peru, LatAm, US) looking for a remote Full Stack developer. They scan fast: role, years, stack, real projects, CV download.
- **Businesses looking for a custom system** (freelance/contract). They want proof that Hector can take an operational problem and ship a working system that runs in production.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Hector Gabriel Almonte Salazar, Systems Engineer and Full Stack Developer based in Ilo, Peru. Success is a visitor who either downloads the CV / reaches out about a role, or sends a project inquiry through the contact form.

## Positioning

Hector builds real industrial and field software, not demo apps:

- Systems in production for hazardous-materials storage, agriculture and industrial safety (Global Plus, Agrosoft, GRE).
- End to end: from idea and design to production, front end + back end + infrastructure, alone.
- Software that works in the field: offline PWAs, OCR from camera, GPS tracking, under pressure.
- Supervision and leadership: currently Systems & Operations Supervisor, not only a coder.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, job applications and direct links. Spanish is the default language; English is fully supported (`/es/`, `/en/`). CV is downloadable as PDF in both languages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro 5 + Tailwind 3 + GSAP, static, deployed on Vercel.
- Domain: **hectoralmonte.com** (purchased; DNS still to be pointed to Vercel).
- Contact form posts to Web3Forms (submissions go to Hector's Gmail).
- Routes: home, about, projects, project detail (`/projects/[slug]`), contact, 404.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim points to a real project, metric or role.
2. A recruiter must get role, stack and CV within one screen.
3. Bilingual parity: nothing ships in only one language.
4. Personality through the art, clarity through the content.
